Hey there! I'm a Indie Puzzle Game Developer, you also can call me PuzzleIdea. Of course — I also see myself as a Game Scholar enthusiast. I believe games are both a way for people to understand the world and a reflection of how we think. From this perspective, and inspired by previous research, I’m interested in exploring non-commercial game design and thinking about what role games play in shaping contemporary society. 你好呀！我是一名解谜游戏开发者，你也可以叫我PuzzleIdea。当然我还有另一个身份-游戏研究的爱好者。我将游戏认为是人类认知世界的手段和思想活动的产物，并以此为基础，结合前人的研究成果，研究非商业的电子游戏设计，以及电子游戏对于当今人类社会发展起到何种作用
